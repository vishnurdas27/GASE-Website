Approaches

Four lenses on performance

We tailor the comparison to the question you need answered.

Internal benchmarking

Compare plants, units, or teams within your own organization to spread proven practice and lift every site to the level of your best.

Competitive benchmarking

Measure directly against sector peers and rivals to understand your market position and defend or extend your lead.

Functional benchmarking

Compare a specific function — supply chain, quality, HR — against strong performers in any industry doing it exceptionally well.

Best-in-class benchmarking

Aim at world-class role models and award-winners to set the highest bar and define what truly great looks like.
